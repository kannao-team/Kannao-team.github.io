Hands Data

We pay craftspeople, starting with tailors, to wear a head camera while they work. We label the footage and sell it to AI and robotics companies. The craftspeople share in the income.

Where we are

Hands Data is at the idea stage. Next, we are interviewing tailors to learn how they work and what they would want from this.
